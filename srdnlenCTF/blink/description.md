# Description

The police gave us a mysterious microcontroller, a STM32F446RE, retrieved in a big operation. They don't know exactly how it works but a mole got a piece of its source code. Can you help them to solve the case?

## Note

- This is a hardware challenge, you have to interact with the board
- To do so you have to book your spot and you have 10 minutes there
- You can go to the board as many times as you like but you can't book two adjacent time intervals
- You can only interact with the board with your hand or using a logic analyzer provided by the staff
- No ST-Link or other external hardware devices are allowed
- Please, go only when you think you know what to do to not waste your and other's time
