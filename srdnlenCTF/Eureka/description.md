# Description

Recently my friend started learning about block ciphers. He's convinced it's not a big problem to do some little changes since it's all super secure. I know he's wrong but he's also very stubborn so, to show me he's right, he prepared a custom algorithm to put in a FPGA and he asked me to try to break it. I'm not the best crypter so I need help, can you recover the flag?

## Note

- This is a hardware challenge, you have to interact with the board
- To do so you have to book your spot and you have 10 minutes there
- You can go to the board as many times as you like but you can't book two adjacent time intervals
- You can only interact with the board with your hand or using a serial adapter provided by the staff
- No other external hardware devices are allowed
- Please, go only when you think you know what to do to not waste your and other's time
